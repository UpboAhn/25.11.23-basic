# QSPR-Informed Minimal PBPK-PINN for Inverse Parameter Identification

A hybrid **Quantitative Structure-Property Relationship (QSPR) + Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN)** framework for predicting multi-organ antibiotic distribution (Plasma, Lung/ELF, and CSF) and inversely identifying unmodeled physiological parameters from sparse clinical data.

---

## 1. Motivation & Problem Statement

In clinical pharmacometrics, predicting drug distribution at infection sites—such as epithelial lining fluid (ELF) in pneumonia or cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) in meningitis—is critical for dose optimization. 

* **Limitation of 2D QSPR:** Physics-based approximations from 2D molecular descriptors (MW, LogP, TPSA via RDKit) capture baseline passive diffusion but fail to predict active or pathological mechanisms, such as **lysosomal trapping** of dibasic macrolides (e.g., Azithromycin) or **inflammation-induced BBB disruption** in meningitis.
* **Limitation of Pure Data-Driven ML:** Clinical sampling of Lung/ELF and CSF is highly invasive and sparse ($n < 10$), causing standard neural networks (MLPs) to severely overfit and fail in extrapolation.
* **Proposed Approach:** We initialize tissue-to-plasma partition coefficients ($K_p$) and BBB permeability ($PS_{\text{BBB}}$) using RDKit 2D descriptors as informative priors, then embed a **4-compartment Minimal PBPK ODE system** into a PINN to inversely identify the true physiological parameters from noisy, sparse observations.

---

## 2. Pipeline Architecture

1. **Molecular Feature Extraction (RDKit):** Parse Canonical SMILES $\rightarrow$ Compute `MW`, `LogP`, `TPSA` $\rightarrow$ Estimate baseline priors ($K_{p,\text{lung}}^{(0)}$, $PS_{\text{BBB}}^{(0)}$).
2. **Physics-Informed Neural Network (PyTorch):**
   * **Input:** Normalized time $\tilde{t} = t / T_{\max} \in [0, 1]$
   * **Output:** Concentration vector $\hat{\mathbf{C}}(\tilde{t}) = [\hat{C}_p, \hat{C}_{\text{lung}}, \hat{C}_{\text{rest}}, \hat{C}_{\text{CSF}}]$
   * **Hard Initial Condition:** $\hat{\mathbf{C}}(\tilde{t}) = \text{Softplus}(\text{MLP}(\tilde{t})) \cdot \tilde{t} \odot \mathbf{s}$ (guarantees $\hat{\mathbf{C}}(0) = \mathbf{0}$ and non-negativity).
   * **Log-Space Reparameterization:** $\theta = \exp(\tilde{\theta})$ to strictly enforce positivity of $K_{p,\text{lung}}$ and $PS_{\text{BBB}}$.

---

## 3. Mathematical Formulation

### Governing Equations (4-Compartment Minimal PBPK)
For an IV infusion rate $R_{\text{inf}}(t)$ (smoothed via sigmoid to preserve differentiability):

$$V_p \frac{dC_p}{dt} = R_{\text{inf}}(t) - CL \cdot C_p - Q_{\text{lung}}\left(C_p - \frac{C_{\text{lung}}}{K_{p,\text{lung}}}\right) - Q_{\text{rest}}\left(C_p - \frac{C_{\text{rest}}}{K_{p,\text{rest}}}\right) - PS_{\text{BBB}}(f_u C_p - C_{\text{CSF}})$$

$$V_{\text{lung}} \frac{dC_{\text{lung}}}{dt} = Q_{\text{lung}}\left(C_p - \frac{C_{\text{lung}}}{K_{p,\text{lung}}}\right)$$

$$V_{\text{rest}} \frac{dC_{\text{rest}}}{dt} = Q_{\text{rest}}\left(C_p - \frac{C_{\text{rest}}}{K_{p,\text{rest}}}\right)$$

$$V_{\text{CSF}} \frac{dC_{\text{CSF}}}{dt} = PS_{\text{BBB}}(f_u C_p - C_{\text{CSF}}) - Q_{\text{CSF}} C_{\text{CSF}}$$

### Composite Loss Function
Because $C_{\text{rest}}$ is clinically unobservable, $\mathcal{L}_{\text{data}}$ is computed solely on Plasma, Lung/ELF, and CSF, while $C_{\text{rest}}$ is implicitly inferred via mass conservation in $\mathcal{L}_{\text{physics}}$:

$$\mathcal{L}(\phi, \theta) = \mathcal{L}_{\text{data}}(\phi) + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{\text{physics}}(\phi, \theta)$$

To resolve multi-scale stiffness across compartments (e.g., $C_{\text{lung}} \gg C_{\text{CSF}}$), each residual term is normalized by its characteristic compartment scale $s_i$.

## 5. Key Numerical Insights & Engineering Notes

* **Stiffness & Residual Scaling:** The fast perfusion rate in the lung ($Q_{\text{lung}}/V_{\text{lung}} = 600\text{ h}^{-1}$) versus slow CSF turnover ($Q_{\text{CSF}}/V_{\text{CSF}} \approx 0.13\text{ h}^{-1}$) creates a stiff ODE system. Without compartment-wise residual scaling, gradients from the lung ODE dominate and prevent CSF convergence.
* **Non-Uniform Collocation:** Concentrating collocation points during the infusion phase ($t \in [0, 2]\text{ h}$) significantly reduced peak concentration ($C_{\max}$) estimation error compared to uniform sampling.

---

## 6. Limitations & Future Work

1. **Inter-Individual Variability (IIV):** The current model fits a single virtual patient. Future work will extend this to **Bayesian PINNs** or **Nonlinear Mixed-Effects (NLME)** frameworks to quantify population uncertainty.
2. **Active Transport Mechanisms:** Replacing the fixed passive permeability term with a **Universal Differential Equation (UDE)** sub-network to learn unknown efflux transporter kinetics (e.g., P-gp at the BBB).
